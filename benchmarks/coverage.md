## Rebuild and interpretation

Run `python3 tools/update_benchmark_index.py` after adding a completed report or changing sources. See the [benchmark guide](README.md) for reproduction and the [verification guide](../docs/guides/verification.md) for input contracts and the limits of checker evidence.

Selection policies differ across records. The original record times two largest inputs per problem; the expanded record prefers three generator families and excludes no-output workloads from timing. Consult the individual report and input hashes before comparing results.
